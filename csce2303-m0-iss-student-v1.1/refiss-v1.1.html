    refiss v1.1. The instruction semantics come from CortexM0Core in rp2040js (MIT licence, © 2021 Uri Shaked), with fixes for shift carry flags, ADC/SBC overflow and BLX LR; the rest implements the CSCE 2303 handout. In this page a run stops after --max-instr instructions; the grader allows 100,000,000.
